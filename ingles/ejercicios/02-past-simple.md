# Ejercicio 2 – Past Simple

## A. Escribe el pasado
1. go → ______
2. study → ______
3. eat → ______
4. stop → ______
5. buy → ______

## B. Completa las oraciones
1. Yesterday I ______ (visit) my grandmother.
2. She ______ (not / go) to the party.
3. ______ you ______ (see) the game last night?
4. We ______ (have) pizza for dinner.
5. They ______ (arrive) two hours ago.

<details>
<summary>Ver respuestas</summary>

**A.** went · studied · ate · stopped · bought

**B.**
1. visited
2. didn't go
3. Did … see
4. had
5. arrived
