# Ejercicio 1 – Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Completa con la forma correcta del verbo entre paréntesis.

1. I usually ______ (walk) to school.
2. Look! The baby ______ (sleep).
3. She ______ (not / like) coffee.
4. ______ you ______ (study) right now?
5. My brother ______ (play) football every Saturday.
6. We ______ (watch) a movie at the moment.
7. He ______ (not / work) today because he is sick.
8. ______ your mother ______ (speak) English?

<details>
<summary>Ver respuestas</summary>

1. walk
2. is sleeping
3. doesn't like
4. Are … studying
5. plays
6. are watching
7. isn't working
8. Does … speak
