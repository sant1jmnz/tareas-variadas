# Vocabulario – 20 verbos frecuentes

|---|---|---|
| to be | ser / estar | I am happy. |
| to have | tener | She has two brothers. |
| to do | hacer | I do my homework. |
| to make | hacer / fabricar | He makes breakfast. |
| to go | ir | We go to school. |
| to get | conseguir / llegar | I get home at 5. |
| to know | saber / conocer | I know the answer. |
| to think | pensar | I think it's true. |
| to take | tomar / llevar | Take an umbrella. |
| to see | ver | I see the problem. |
| to come | venir | Come here, please. |
| to want | querer | I want some water. |
| to look | mirar / parecer | Look at this! |
| to use | usar | Use a pencil. |
| to find | encontrar | I can't find my keys. |
| to give | dar | Give me the book. |
| to tell | decir / contar | Tell me a story. |
| to work | trabajar / funcionar | My phone doesn't work. |
| to call | llamar | Call me later. |
| to try | intentar / probar | Try again. |
