# Present Simple

**Uso:** hábitos, rutinas y hechos generales.

| Forma | Estructura | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Afirmativa | sujeto + verbo (+ **s** con he/she/it) | She **works** every day. |
| Negativa | sujeto + do/does + **not** + verbo | He **doesn't** work on Sundays. |
| Pregunta | Do/Does + sujeto + verbo? | **Do** you study English? |

**Palabras clave:** always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day.
