# Present Continuous

**Uso:** acciones que pasan ahora mismo o planes ya acordados para pronto.

| Forma | Estructura | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Afirmativa | sujeto + am/is/are + verbo**-ing** | I **am studying** now. |
| Negativa | sujeto + am/is/are + **not** + verbo-ing | She **isn't watching** TV. |
| Pregunta | Am/Is/Are + sujeto + verbo-ing? | **Are** you **listening**? |

**Reglas del -ing:**
- Casi todos: + ing → read → read**ing**
- Termina en -e: quita la e → write → writ**ing**
- Consonante-vocal-consonante (sílaba fuerte): dobla la última → run → run**ning**, swim → swim**ming**

**Palabras clave:** now, right now, at the moment, today, look!, listen!

> ⚠️ Verbos de estado (know, like, want, need, understand) casi nunca van en -ing:
> ✅ I **know** the answer. ❌ I am knowing the answer.
