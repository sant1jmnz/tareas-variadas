# Lectura 1 – My Daily Routine (nivel básico)

My name is Laura and I am fifteen years old. I wake up at six thirty every morning.
First, I take a shower and get dressed. Then I have breakfast with my family. I usually
eat bread and drink orange juice.

I go to school by bus. My classes start at eight o'clock. My favorite subject is English
because the teacher is fun and we play games. After school, I do my homework and I
sometimes play basketball with my friends.

In the evening, I have dinner at seven. After dinner, I watch TV or read a book.
I go to bed at ten o'clock. Today is Saturday, so I am not going to school. Right now
I am writing this text!

## Preguntas
1. How old is Laura?
2. What does she eat for breakfast?
3. How does she go to school?
4. Why does she like English?
5. What is she doing right now?
