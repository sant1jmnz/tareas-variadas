🔄 Passive Voice

La voz pasiva se usa cuando lo importante es la acción o el objeto, no quién la hace (porque no lo sabemos, es obvio o no importa).

Activa vs. pasiva

ActivaPasiva
James Cameron directed Titanic.Titanic was directed by James Cameron.
Someone stole my bike.My bike was stolen.
Estructura: objeto + be (en el tiempo del verbo original) + participio pasado (+ by + quién lo hace)

El truco: el verbo be toma el tiempo verbal de la oración activa, y el verbo principal siempre va en participio (3ª columna).

La pasiva en cada tiempo verbal

TiempoForma de beActivaPasiva
Present simpleam / is / are + ppThey make films here.Films are made here.
Present continuousam / is / are being + ppThey are repairing the cinema.The cinema is being repaired.
Past simplewas / were + ppNolan directed Inception.Inception was directed by Nolan.
Past continuouswas / were being + ppThey were filming a scene.A scene was being filmed.
Present perfecthas / have been + ppThey have cancelled the show.The show has been cancelled.
Past perfecthad been + ppThey had sold all the tickets.All the tickets had been sold.
Future (will)will be + ppThey will announce the winners.The winners will be announced.
going toam/is/are going to be + ppThey are going to release a sequel.A sequel is going to be released.
Modalesmodal + be + ppYou must turn off your phone.Your phone must be turned off.

Negativas y preguntas

Se forman con el verbo be, igual que siempre:

  • The film wasn't released in Argentina.
  • Was the film directed by Spielberg?
  • The tickets haven't been sold yet.
  • Is English spoken in Canada?
✅ ¿Cuándo uso "by"?Solo cuando importa quién hizo la acción. No se pone by people, by someone, by them: ❌ My phone was stolen by someone. → ✅ My phone was stolen.
⚠️ Errores típicos ❌ The film directed by Nolan. → ✅ The film was directed by Nolan. (¡falta be!)
❌ The house was build in 1990. → ✅ The house was built in 1990. (participio, no infinitivo)

🕵️ Modals of Deduction

Usamos estos modales para sacar conclusiones o decir qué tan seguros estamos de algo, a partir de lo que vemos o sabemos. Como un detective.

En presente

mustEstoy casi seguro de que sí (95%)
might / may / couldEs posible (50%)
can'tEstoy casi seguro de que no (95%)
sujeto + must / might / may / could / can't + verbo base
  • She's been working all day. She must be tired. (seguro que está cansada)
  • I don't know where Tom is. He might be at the cinema. (puede ser)
  • You've just eaten a huge pizza. You can't be hungry! (imposible)

Para algo que está pasando ahora, usamos be + -ing:

  • The lights are off. They must be sleeping.
  • He's not answering. He might be watching a film.

En pasado

sujeto + must / might / may / could / can't / couldn't + have + participio
ModalSignificadoEjemplo
must have + ppseguro que pasóThe streets are wet. It must have rained.
might / may / could have + ppquizás pasóShe's late. She might have missed the bus.
can't / couldn't have + ppimposible que haya pasadoHe can't have seen the film. It isn't out yet.
⚠️ Errores típicos ❌ He mustn't be at home. → ✅ He can't be at home. (mustn't es prohibición, no deducción)
❌ It can be true. → ✅ It might / could be true. (para posibilidad no se usa can)
❌ She must to be tired. → ✅ She must be tired. (después de un modal, nunca to)
❌ He must have went. → ✅ He must have gone. (participio)
✅ Truco para reconocerlosSi en español dirías "seguro que…" → must. "Capaz que / quizás…" → might. "No puede ser que…" → can't.

🔀 First Conditional

Se usa para hablar de situaciones reales o posibles en el futuro y su resultado probable.

If + present simple,  will + verbo base
  • If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.
  • If you study, you'll pass the exam.
  • If she doesn't hurry, she won't catch the bus.

Las dos partes se pueden invertir. Si el if va primero, se pone coma; si va en el medio, no:

  • We'll stay at home if it rains.
⚠️ El error más comúnNunca va will después de if:
❌ If it will rain, we'll stay home. → ✅ If it rains, we'll stay home.

Variantes

En lugar de will…Ejemplo
may / might (menos seguro)If you go to bed late, you might feel tired.
can (permiso / posibilidad)If you finish your homework, you can watch TV.
imperativo (orden / consejo)If you see Tom, tell him to call me.
going toIf they lose again, the coach is going to quit.

unless, when, as soon as

  • unless = if … not (a menos que): Unless you study, you'll fail. = If you don't study, you'll fail.
  • when = estás seguro de que va a pasar: I'll call you when I get home. (seguro llego)
  • if = puede pasar o no: I'll call you if I have time. (no sé si voy a tener)
  • as soon as / before / after / until también van con presente: I'll text you as soon as I arrive.
⚠️ Ojo con unlessUnless ya es negativo: ❌ Unless you don't study… → ✅ Unless you study…
✅ Comparalo con el Zero ConditionalZero (verdades generales): If you heat ice, it melts. (siempre pasa)
First (una situación concreta del futuro): If you leave the ice outside, it will melt.
